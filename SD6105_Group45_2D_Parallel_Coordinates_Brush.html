Reading the chart: each colored line is one movie; each vertical axis represents a different metric. Axis ticks use readable units ($M, $B, %, and rating out of 10). Revenue, budget and popularity use logarithmic scales; ROI uses a symmetric nonlinear display scale to accommodate extreme values. Values outside the displayed limits are clipped; hover over the lines near an axis to inspect exact values. ROI proxy = (revenue − budget) / budget × 100%; it is not net profit. Genre = the first listed TMDB genre. No animation.
Tip: Click and drag UP or DOWN on a vertical axis to select a range (brush). Selected movies stay bright; others fade. Brush multiple axes to intersect conditions. Drag again to replace a selection; click an axis to clear its brush. Hover near an axis for film details..
